# Diving Palau

Bumphead Parrotfish (Bolbometopon muricatum) Spawning occurs monthly 2 - 3 days before the new moon. Hundreds of fish gather and sub-groups rush toward the surface releaseing a cloud of eggs and sperm.

| Dive Site                          | Max Depth | Overview & Highlights                                                                                                                |
| :--------------------------------- | :-------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Jake Seaplane                      | 9 - 15 m  | Imperial Japanese Navy Aichi E13A-1.                                                                                                 |
| Siaes Corner & Tunnel              | 24 m +    | Drift dive drop-off.                                                                                                                 |
| IJN Iro                            | 9 - 40 m  | Bow & Stern Deck Guns. Kingposts & Crossbraces.                                                                                      |
| Ulong Channel                      | 9 - 14 m  | Drift dive / Reef hook dive.                                                                                                         |
| **Blue Corner & Blue Holes**       | 5 - 26 m  | Drift dive / Reef hook dive. Grey reef sharks, whitetip reef sharks, schools of chevron barracudas, bigeye jacks, and dogtooth tuna. |
| German Channel                     | 9 - 24 m  | Blasted out during German colonial period in 1908 to allow boats to transport phosphate from Peleliu to Koror. Manta feeding ground. |
| **Peleliu Express & Peleliu Wall** | 7 m +     | Drift dive along the southern tip of Peleliu Island.                                                                                 |
| Turtle Cove                        | 3 - 18 m  | Green sea turtles and hawksbill turtles.                                                                                             |
| Chandelier Cave                    | 4 - 11 m  | Five interconnected chambers, air pockets and stalactites. Malakal Harbor mandarinfish feeding ground.                               |
