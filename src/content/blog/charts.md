Maps show static terrain and landmarks, whereas charts provide navigational data like controlled airspace boundaries, radio frequencies and VFR reporting points.

## Important Charts

| Abbreviation | Full Name                | Purpose & Coverage                                                   |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PCA          | Planning Chart Australia | A single large-scale chart covering the entire Australian continent. |
| VNC          | Visual Navigation Chart  | VFR (Visual Flight Rules) navigation (1:500,000).                    |
| VTC          | Visual Terminal Chart    | VFR navigation (1:250,000).                                          |

## Other charts:

| Abbreviation | Full Name                   | Purpose & Coverage                                      |
| ------------ | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| ERC High     | En Route Chart (High Level) | High-altitude IFR (Instrument Flight Rules) flights.    |
| ERC Low      | En Route Chart (Low Level)  | Low-altitude IFR flights.                               |
| TAC          | Terminal Area Chart         | Controlled airspace around major capital city airports. |
