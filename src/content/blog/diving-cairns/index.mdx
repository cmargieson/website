# Diving Cairns

Diving Flynn and Milln Reefs reveals widespread bleaching, storm rubble, and Crown-of-Thorns starfish damage, leaving both sites in poor condition. Marine life was sparse, with stretches of bare reef and few fish in sight.

| Parent Reef | Dive Site        | Max Depth | Overview & Highlights                                                              |
| :---------- | :--------------- | :-------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Milln Reef  | Whale Bommie     | 22–26m    | Giant standalone coral pinnacle with caves, ledges, and deep walls.                |
| Milln Reef  | Petaj Bommie     | 14m       | Shallow coral heads over sandy patches.                                            |
| Flynn Reef  | Gordon's Mooring | 22m       | Swim-throughs, overhangs, and a collection of smaller bommies. Giant clam gardens. |
| Flynn Reef  | Tracy's Bommie   | 18–22m    | Large pinnacle dropping off into a deep outer wall. Reef sharks.                   |
| Flynn Reef  | Little Tracy's   | 12–18m    | Sloping wall and hard plate coral gardens, staghorn corals.                        |
